## Q1: [Traffic Accidents] For Overseas Chinese: What is insider trading crime and how to defend against it?

**A**: Insider trading crime refers to insiders or those illegally obtaining inside information trading securities before information is public. Defense points: 1) Whether information qualifies as statutory inside information; 2) Whether defendant is statutory insider; 3) Causal relationship between trading and inside information.

**法律依据**: 《证券法》第76条

**领域**: 证券犯罪 | **语言**: English

## Q2: [Securities Crime] For Overseas Chinese: What compliance requirements apply to cross-border e-commerce platforms?

**A**: Cross-border e-commerce compliance: 1) Customs registration and e-commerce enterprise filing; 2) Products must meet Chinese quality standards; 3) Tax compliance including customs duty, VAT, and consumption tax; 4) Consumer protection including 7-day return policy; 5) Data compliance under Data Security Law and PIPL.

**法律依据**: 《跨境电子商务零售进口商品清单》

**领域**: 跨境电商 | **语言**: English
